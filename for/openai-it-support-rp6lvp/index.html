Turn the top repeat tickets into guided self-service

Your posting asks for repeatable workflows. I would find the ten requests that come back most often and write a guided fix for each, the way Interloper walks a user through one.

A closing habit: problem, cause, fix, how to confirm. A short template keeps it under two minutes, and the knowledge base grows from real work instead of a documentation sprint that never comes.
