Leadership under pressure

A practical, scenario-based microlearning experience on high-stakes communication, decision clarity, and operational handoffs.

15-minute self-paced demo
3 decision scenarios
Knowledge check + feedback
Learning goal

When information is incomplete or time-sensitive, choose communication and escalation behaviors that protect safety, accountability, and service quality.

Fictional scenarios; created to demonstrate instructional design. Not JPMorganChase training or an employer-endorsed course.

How it works

Read the situation, select one response, and see a specific explanation. Each decision is scored against the stated behavioral criteria.

Learning objectives

  1. Identify missing information before acting.
  2. Escalate time-sensitive risk using clear facts.
  3. Close the loop with ownership and follow-up.

Design approach: contextual decisions, immediate explanatory feedback, transfer-to-work reflection, and a measurable completion check.
